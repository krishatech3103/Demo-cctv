Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding installation, warranty, or mobile setup? We've got answers.

How long does CCTV camera installation take?
Standard 4 to 8 camera installations for retail shops and homes are completed within 4 to 6 hours by our certified technicians.
Can I watch camera footage live on my mobile phone?
Yes! We configure your DVR/NVR with secure P2P cloud streaming so you can watch live video and receive push notifications on your phone.
What warranty do you provide on CCTV cameras?
All CCTV cameras, DVRs, and hard disks come with official manufacturer warranty along with local service support.
Do your cameras record audio as well?
Yes, our HD Built-In Mic cameras record clear audio along with 4K video, ideal for cash counters and office reception desks.
